This Privacy Policy applies specifically to the TikTok Mini Game ‘3D Car Run’ published by YBDStudio.

3D Car Run is a puzzle game developed and published by YBD Studio, a game publishing brand operated under Designfullprints LLC.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect as little data as possible.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you play our game.

By using our games, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information Collected Automatically

When you use the game, we may collect limited non-personal information, including:

  • Device type and operating system
  • Anonymous device identifiers
  • Gameplay events (such as session start/end, level progress, crashes)
  • Ad interaction data (e.g. rewarded ads viewed)

This information does not identify you personally.

b) Advertising Data

3D Car Run may display ads provided by third-party advertising partners.

Ad partners may collect:

  • Anonymous identifiers
  • Ad impressions and interactions
  • Approximate location (country/region only)

This data is used to:

  • Show relevant ads
  • Measure ad performance
  • Prevent fraud

c) Push Notifications

If you choose to enable notifications, we may send gameplay-related messages such as:

  • Reminders to continue playing
  • Notifications about new content or updates

You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Precise location data
  • Contact lists
  • Photos, videos, or microphone data
  • Any form of payment or financial information

3. How We Use Information

We use collected data only to:

  • Improve gameplay experience
  • Fix bugs and crashes
  • Analyze gameplay balance and difficulty
  • Deliver optional ads
  • Send optional notifications (if enabled)

4. Third-Party Services

3D Car Run uses third-party services for:

  • Analytics
  • Advertising
  • Push notifications

These services may collect data according to their own privacy policies.

We only work with providers that comply with applicable data protection laws.

5. Children’s Privacy

3D Car Run does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal data, please contact us and we will remove it promptly.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access or misuse.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to data collected about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of personalized ads (where applicable)

You can exercise these rights by contacting us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
